Lesson 01 · Internet basics

How does the internet move information?

Think of the internet as a network of networks that helps devices communicate.

  1. Your device makes a request. For example, you open a website in a browser.
  2. Data travels across connected networks. Routers help direct information toward its destination.
  3. The response returns to your device. Your browser uses the received data to show the page.
Remember: The internet is the network. The web is one of the services that uses it.
