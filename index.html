🎮 All About Computer Games

What are Computer Games?

Computer games are interactive forms of entertainment played on a computer or other digital device. You control characters or elements in a virtual world to achieve goals or compete with others. They come in many types, from simple puzzles to big adventures!

Proper care helps your computer work well for a long time. It prevents damage, protects your files, and saves you from problems later. A happy computer is a healthy computer!

🌐 Welcome to The Internet

What is the Internet?

The Internet is a giant, worldwide network connecting millions of computers. Think of it like a huge web that lets computers talk to each other and share information from all over the planet!

How Does it Work?

Information is broken into small pieces called packets. These packets travel across the network through devices called routers and are put back together at their destination.

What Can We Do on the Internet?

  • Communicate: Send emails, chat with friends, and use social media.
  • Learn: Find information for homework and learn new things on websites like Wikipedia.
  • Have Fun: Watch videos, listen to music, and play online games.
  • Shop: Buy things from online stores.
